Paleolithic era

Humans have had a relationship with caves since the beginning of our evolution, with many of our ancestors calling them home.

With the discovery of fire, prehistoric humans were able to venture deeper into caves. Nomadic hunter-gatherer groups used them as temporary shelters, sources of materials, and canvases for art. In the undisturbed underground, we can still see evidence of their existence in footprints in the dust and painted handprints.

Further along in history, caves started to become used as permanent homes. Civilizations would take great effort in carving and decorating them to be comfortable, creating cave-cities that still invoke awe today.
