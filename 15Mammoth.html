Mammoth Cave

Location: Kentucky, USA

Known for: World’s longest known cave system

The Mammoth Cave is a sprawling maze of caves and underground passages, reaching up to at least 560 km, with more yet to be explored. It contains almost every type of cave formation– including cracks and fissures, underground rivers, vertical climbs, horizontal crawls, and more– as well as a diverse ecosystem of cave flora and fauna. By analysing its walls, we are able to learn about the geological history of the world. Since 1981, it has been designated as a UNESCO World Heritage site.

The Mammoth Cave is one of the most explored caves, offering everything from easy passages for tourists guided by the National Park Service, harder exploration for cavers wanting a challenge, and a wealth of knowledge for researchers.
